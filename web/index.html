What Buy and Strong Buy mean

Stocks: Buy = 1 of 3 dip signals, Strong Buy = 2 or more. Crypto: Buy = 3 of 4 trend signals with none against, Strong Buy = all 4. Tested over 10 years: Strong Buys did clearly better; single-signal Buys were close to random. Alerts go out for Strong Buys.
